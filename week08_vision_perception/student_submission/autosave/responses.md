## concepts.prediction
Classical perception uses simple rules like color, size, and shape to find objects. Learned perception uses AI to recognize objects. Dim light makes objects harder to see, especially for classical detection. Glare can mess up both methods. Distance makes objects look smaller, so both may miss them. Occlusion means an object is partly hidden, which is harder for classical detection, while AI may still recognize it. Rotation means an object is turned, which can confuse classical detection, but AI may still recognize it. Clutter means too many objects are in the background, which can confuse both methods. A similar-looking distractor is an object that looks like the target. Classical detection may mistake it for the target, while AI may be better at telling them apart.

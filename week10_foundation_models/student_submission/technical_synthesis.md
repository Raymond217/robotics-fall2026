When doing the three missions, I learned that the programming models can be useful, but their answers and decisions are not always reliable. In mission 1 the robot cannot physically pick up or carry objects because it did not have an arm or gripper. In mission 2, the model incorrectly identified a compass as tape measure. It showed that AI can misunderstand visual information. In mission 3, the robot struggled to navigate around walls. The goal only prompt resulted in a few blocked move and never managed to reach the goal.
